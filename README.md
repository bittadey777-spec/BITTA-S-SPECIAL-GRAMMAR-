# BITTA'S SPECIAL
**১৮-১৯ পর্যন্ত ভালোভাবে চলবেই**

Class 7 English Grammar (Bangladesh NCTB Curriculum) Android app with bilingual Bengali & English lessons, interactive MCQ & Fill-in-the-Blank quizzes, Writing/Composition guides, and local progress tracking.

## 📲 Download APK (Android)

You can download the installable debug APK directly from this repository:

👉 **[Download `app-debug.apk` (18.43 MB)](./APK_DOWNLOAD/app-debug.apk)**

### How to Install on Android
1. Tap the link above (or open `APK_DOWNLOAD/app-debug.apk` and click **Raw / Download**).
2. Open the downloaded `.apk` file on your Android phone.
3. If prompted, allow **Install from Unknown Sources** for your browser/file manager.
4. Tap **Install**.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Right Forms of Verbs** (7 rules + 10 interactive questions)
- **Narration — Direct & Indirect Speech** (7 rules + 10 interactive questions)
- **Changing Sentences & Voice Change** (6 rules + 6 interactive questions)
- **Articles & Prepositions** (6 rules + 6 interactive questions)
- **Completing Sentences & Suffix-Prefix** (6 rules + 6 interactive questions)
- **Paragraph, Application, Dialogue & Composition Guides** (model samples + vocabulary + format quiz)
- **Bilingual Support:** Clear Bengali (`বাংলা`) and English explanations with instant quiz feedback and local score tracking.
